Transfers outside Japan

This page explains transfers of information outside Japan: the destination country, that country's rules, and the safeguards at the recipient.

There is no contract on the handling of personal information between Kotoba Labs and the recipients below. Each recipient handles information under its own privacy policy.

You can withdraw permission in Settings › Allowed sending in the app. Withdrawing does not affect sending that already took place.

アメリカ合衆国

国名: アメリカ合衆国。

その国の制度: 個人情報保護委員会の「外国における個人情報の保護に関する制度等の調査」の報告がある。連邦の報告が中心になる。ニューヨーク州の報告もある（Hugging Face の利用規約の準拠法はニューヨーク州法）。

  • ニューヨーク州: newyork_report.pdf

United States of America

Country: United States of America.

That country's rules: The Personal Information Protection Commission publishes survey reports on foreign data protection systems. The federal report applies. A New York report is also available (Hugging Face's terms are governed by New York law).

  • New York: newyork_report.pdf
